Trail running

Learn to move well on the trails

Two seven-week courses on the North Shore, built around chi running technique and run in a fun, supportive group.

7 weeks

Introductory Course

Wednesdays at 9:00am

New to trail running, or just want to build your fitness? This is an introduction to the techniques and skills you need to become a trail runner, in a fun and supportive environment.

Absolute beginners are welcome and no experience is necessary.

7 weeks

Intermediate Course

Tuesdays at 9:00am

For you if you can already run three to five kilometres and want to bring that onto the trail, develop it further, or get back into trail running after a break.

What you'll work on

Each week explores a new trail

  • Chi running technique
  • Strategies for uphill and downhill
  • Managing more technical trails
  • Trail etiquette
  • Breathing and posture
  • How to avoid injuries

Group fitness

Cardio and strength, outdoors, all year

On the beach at Dundarave and Ambleside, and along the Ambleside seawall. All fitness levels are welcome.

Drop in or 6 weeks

Group Fitness at the beach

Thursdays at 9:15am · Dundarave or Ambleside

Cardio and strength based activities on the sand, with all fitness levels welcome. Come as a drop-in or join the six-week course.

And perhaps you'll join us for a refreshing swim afterwards.

6 weeks

Learn to run 5km

Thursdays at 5:00pm · Ambleside seawall

Develop your technique and your stamina, working towards an event or a goal of your own. Complete two short homework sessions each week and you will get to five kilometres.

Perhaps the Ambleside Parkrun is for you afterwards.

One to one

Personal Coaching

Parks, beach, trails, or your own backyard

Outdoor personal training with a certified coach, blending the customised programming and accountability of traditional training with the mental health and cardiovascular benefits of exercising in nature.

Good for building back after a health challenge, improving your running technique, training for an event, or simply having someone in your corner.
